# MarketOps 360

**A connected regional specialty-grocery operating model — from customer experience to enterprise execution.**

MarketOps 360 is an interactive business simulation designed to show how a growing regional specialty-grocery retailer can operate as one connected business.

Rather than presenting isolated dashboards, the demo connects **product, merchandising, suppliers, procurement, distribution, receiving, inventory, stores, workforce, quality, customer service, finance, expansion and technology delivery** through shared business flows.

The business content, scale assumptions and operating records are fictional and illustrative. The model is intentionally sized and structured to represent the complexity of a growing regional specialty-grocery operator with multiple stores, distribution capability and a few hundred represented employees.

---

## 1. The Business Model

The simulated company operates a regional network of specialty-grocery stores supported by:

- Multiple retail stores
- Regional distribution centers
- Buyers and merchandising teams
- Supplier relationships
- Fresh and prepared-food operations
- Store operations teams
- Workforce planning
- Quality and food-safety controls
- Finance and commercial governance
- Technology delivery
- New-store expansion
- Customer service and digital commerce

The central idea is simple:

> **A decision made in one part of the business should create the appropriate operational consequence in another part of the business.**

For example:

**Customer demand → inventory signal → buying decision → purchase order → supplier → distribution → receiving → inventory → store availability → customer experience → financial result**

That connected flow is the core of MarketOps 360.

# 6. Cross-Functional Cause and Effect

The most important characteristic of the simulation is that actions are not intended to remain isolated.

Examples include:

### Customer purchase

Customer checkout  
→ order created  
→ shared inventory decreases  
→ availability changes

### Purchase order

PO created  
→ inbound shipment created  
→ distribution activity appears  
→ receiving can act on the shipment

### Receiving

Shipment received  
→ PO status changes  
→ accepted inventory becomes available  
→ downstream replenishment / store activity can change

### Transfer

Transfer created  
→ source inventory decreases  
→ destination inventory increases  
→ movement is recorded

### Product introduction

Product progresses through its lifecycle  
→ downstream commercial activity becomes possible  
→ launch readiness changes

### Store expansion

Expansion progresses through readiness stages  
→ opening status changes  
→ new store becomes part of the operating model

The purpose is not to reproduce a production ERP. The purpose is to demonstrate how an integrated operating model can be represented digitally.

# 9. Additional Business Use Cases

The operating model also represents broader business situations.

### Fresh Produce Replenishment

Demand increases → inventory approaches par → buyer reviews requirement → supplier capacity is checked → PO is created → distribution receives product → store availability is restored.

### Cold-Chain Excursion

Shipment arrives → temperature exception identified → product quarantined → quality disposition required → affected inventory remains unavailable until decision.

### New Store Opening

Expansion project → permits → construction → technology → staffing → initial inventory → readiness review → launch → hypercare.

### Customer Order

Customer places order → inventory is consumed → order is created → store / fulfillment activity follows → customer receives the result.

### Supplier Short Shipment

PO expected quantity differs from received quantity → receiving records variance → PO balance remains open → procurement follows up with supplier.

### Three-Way Match

Purchase order  
+ receipt  
+ invoice  
→ financial reconciliation.

A mismatch creates an exception rather than silently passing through.

### Product Recall

Recall initiated → affected product / lot identified → locations identified → inventory held → store/DC action required → traceability maintained.

### Peak Workforce Planning

Demand forecast → labor requirement → coverage analysis → uncovered hours → staffing action.

### Store-to-Store Transfer

Source inventory checked → transfer requested → quantity validated → source reduced → destination increased → movement recorded.

### Promotion Governance

Promotion proposal → funding / pricing review → margin check → approval or rejection.

### Technology Release

Initiative → build → testing → readiness review → release → hypercare → value measurement.

### Shrink / Waste / Expiry

Inventory exposure → operational review → waste / markdown decision → financial impact → corrective action.

# 16. Why MarketOps 360 Exists

Many enterprise applications represent business functions as separate screens.

Real businesses do not operate that way.

A buyer's decision affects procurement.

Procurement affects distribution.

Distribution affects receiving.

Receiving affects inventory.

Inventory affects stores.

Stores affect customers.

Customers affect sales.

Sales affect finance.

Technology enables and changes many of those processes.

MarketOps 360 was created to make those relationships visible and interactive.

> **The objective is not to simulate every detail of a real retailer.  
> The objective is to demonstrate how a complex business can be understood, connected and translated into a usable digital operating model.**

## QA & UAT Bench — Testing Guide

Choose **Executive**, **IT Program Manager**, **Quality Manager**, **Regional Manager**, or **System Administrator** from the role selector, then open **QA & UAT Bench** in the enterprise navigation.

The UAT register contains **18 previously observed defects** (2 Critical, 9 High and 7 Medium). Each entry contains a responsible role, preconditions, specific steps, the expected result, and what the previous UAT observed. Expand a scenario to review its details. Filter by severity, role, retest outcome or search terms.

Select **Record retest** to save the tester, execution evidence, outcome (**Not retested**, **Pass**, **Fail** or **Blocked**) and defect disposition. **Verified / Closed** is permitted only alongside a passing retest. Results persist only in the current browser tab; they are not uploaded to an external defect-management system. Select **Export UAT CSV** to export the 18 cases with recorded evidence and dispositions, or **Clear retest records** to reset only the retest results.

The separate **Run integrity checks** control validates current simulated business records and is not equivalent to executing all 18 UAT reproduction procedures. Historical findings remain open until individually retested and verified. A complete live-browser UAT, including mobile usage, is still required for final acceptance.

## Smart Action Receipts · RACI Messaging

All 15 demo role experiences now share **Messages & Handoffs** and a dedicated **My Work Queue** page. Each owner sees assigned RACI follow-ups there without waiting for a message or SMS. Successful self-service changes generate an on-screen **Action Receipt** showing the changed business record, the affected areas, and the RACI follow-up:

- **R – Responsible:** Role expected to take the next action.
- **A – Accountable:** Role responsible for oversight and completion.
- **C – Consulted:** Roles that can provide input when needed.
- **I – Informed:** Roles that may need status visibility.

When a next action is required, a traceable handoff automatically appears in the Responsible role's **My Work Queue** and the **Handoffs** tab. The queue shows the originating record, next action, Responsible owner, Accountable role, status and due condition. Completing a linked business action supersedes the prior handoff and moves ownership to the next role; manual handoff closure requires evidence and is labeled distinctly from updating the underlying business record. The receipt also distinguishes **records updated/linked**, **relevant demo roles**, **in-app recipients actually messaged**, and **SMS drafts opened but not confirmed delivered**. Completed workflows explicitly show **Completed — no follow-up required**.

Every action receipt offers two optional communications:

1. **Send message:** Select RACI recipients (Responsible and Accountable by default, excluding the acting role; Consulted and Informed can be selected). The message is delivered to the selected **demo role inboxes in the same browser tab**. Recipients can read messages after switching roles. This does not send an email or notify a real employee.
2. **Send text…:** Prepare a short SMS-style handoff. Enter a real recipient number only if authorized, then **Open SMS app** to review and explicitly send from your own device; or **Copy text** for another channel. MarketOps never sends the SMS itself or claims delivery confirmation. Entered phone numbers are not saved.

Open **Messages & Handoffs** from the sidebar to see **Inbox**, **Sent**, and **Handoffs**. Responsible owners can mark a follow-up complete with evidence. All information is simulated and persists in the current browser tab until the demo is reset or the tab session ends.

**Safety and scope:** Role selection, access restrictions, and role inboxes are simulated, not real user authentication. These communications are **not** a production SMS gateway, email provider, or real employee-notification system. The existing QA & UAT defect register remains unchanged; its open defects are not automatically considered fixed.


### Smart Action Receipt regression journeys

- **Customer → Crew → Customer:** Place an order; Crew receives a traceable next action in My Work Queue. When Crew completes its picking task, that handoff is superseded by a Customer pickup confirmation. Confirmed pickup closes the workflow with no further action.
- **Procurement → Distribution → Store Captain:** Confirm a supplier PO. Distribution receives a queued handoff with the scheduled ASN delivery. Receiving the linked shipment supersedes the PO handoff and assigns the next store-replenishment action to Store Captain.
- **Any successful business change:** Review the on-screen receipt. No one is claimed to have been notified until **Send message** is used, and any **Send text** action prepares an external SMS draft without confirming delivery. Switch to the Responsible role and open My Work Queue to inspect the assigned action and its due information.

QA regression checks cover all 15 queue pages and these linked workflows. The application remains a front-end simulation; it does not provide real authenticated multi-user assignments or an SMS delivery service.
